# Code of conduct

Doseph is for anyone who grows plants or builds things, whatever their
experience or background. This page says how we treat each other in the
issues and pull requests of this repository.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone started with
  a first tank.
- Criticise the code, the design or the docs, not the person. Say what
  is wrong and, if you can, how it could be better.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Stay on topic. Keep in mind that everything here is public.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or attacks on someone's identity,
  background or body.
- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private details (address, location, photos of
  their setup they did not share here) without their consent.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, and repeated off-topic
  posts.
- Encouraging anyone to break the law or to put people at risk, for
  example by bypassing a safety function on real hardware.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, and
block people who break these rules, temporarily or for good. In most
cases a short note comes first.

## Reporting

**Report content** in the ⋯ menu of a comment or issue sends a report to
GitHub's staff
([reporting abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)).
The maintainer reads the issues and acts on what breaks these rules. A
security problem is not a conduct matter: see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

This code applies in all spaces of this repository and to anyone acting
on behalf of the project elsewhere.
